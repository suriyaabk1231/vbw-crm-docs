# 1. Problem and solution

**In one line:** teams find today's CRMs too hard to set up, too costly as they grow, and weak on WhatsApp
and follow-ups. VBW CRM is a simple CRM a hospital, gym, education centre, retail shop or real estate agent can set up in 5 minutes for ₹599 a month.

## What users complain about in today's CRMs

From real user reviews (Capterra, G2, HubSpot community) and published surveys, collected October 2026.

| CRM | Main complaints | Our answer |
|---|---|---|
| Zoho CRM | Complex setup that can need a developer, cluttered screens, confusing permissions, costly as teams grow, slow support on cheaper plans | 5-minute setup, simple screens, only 2 roles, WhatsApp support |
| HubSpot | Large price jump from Starter to Professional, new features in expensive plans, extra packages when limits are hit | One fixed price, nothing locked away |
| Pipedrive | Expensive as teams grow, paid add-ons, weak reporting, some integrations need Zapier | Reports and core features included |
| Bigin (Zoho) | No invoicing, no WhatsApp automation, limited customisation, support takes days | WhatsApp built in, fast support |
| Kylas | Slow with large data | Speed targets and load tests before launch |
| TeleCRM | Too few reviews to judge | — |

Other findings:
- Salespeople spend most of their time on admin instead of selling (Salesforce State of Sales).
- Top reasons businesses don't adopt a CRM: cost, moving old data, getting staff to use it, missing integrations.

## Problems our 3 target industries have

| Industry | Daily problem | VBW CRM solves it with |
|---|---|---|
| Hospitals | Patients forget follow-up visits; appointment book on paper; no-shows not tracked | Appointments, follow-up reminders, no-show tracking, WhatsApp reminders |
| Gyms | Memberships expire without renewal; trial visitors never called back; fees chased by phone | Renewal dates and alerts, trial follow-ups, WhatsApp reminders |
| Education centres | Enquiries lost in WhatsApp; demo classes not followed up; fee instalments paid late | Enquiry pipeline, demo class booking, fee due reminders |
| Retail shops | No record of who bought what; birthdays and festivals pass without an offer; regulars stop coming unnoticed | Purchase history, birthday / anniversary offers, "not back in 60 days" list, WhatsApp offers to customers who agreed |
| Real estate agents | Buyer requirements in notebooks; new listings not sent to the right buyers; site visits and rent renewals missed | Requirement fields, property list matched to clients, one-tap property sharing, site visits, 11-month agreement reminders |
