# 13 — Call script (English + Tamil)

For calling the businesses on the [target list](16-target-list-template.csv). Goal of the first call:
**book a 15-minute visit or video call** to set up the free pilot. Do not try to sell on the first call.

> **Tamil text:** written in spoken Tamil, with the English words owners already use (WhatsApp, follow-up, Excel).
> Ask a native speaker to read it aloud once and adjust the wording to your own way of speaking.

## Before you call

- Call **11 am – 1 pm** or **3 – 5 pm**. Avoid hospital OPD rush, gym peak hours (6–9 am, 6–9 pm) and class times.
- Look at their Google Maps page first: reviews, timings, photos. Use one detail in the call ("I saw your 4.6 rating…").
- Have the target list open. After each call, fill `status`, `next_step` and `next_step_date`.
- Ask for the **owner** (doctor, gym owner, centre head). If you reach the receptionist, be friendly: they are the future daily user.

## What we can honestly promise (do not say more)

| Say | Do not say |
|---|---|
| One place for every patient / member / student, their visits, renewals and fees | "Fully automatic" |
| The dashboard shows who to call or remind today | "Your phone will remind you" (the Android app is not out yet) |
| Ready WhatsApp messages: one tap fills the name, date and amount, and you press Send in your own WhatsApp | "Sends WhatsApp automatically" |
| ₹599 a month + GST for 2 people; 100 days free for pilot businesses, then ₹599 locked for life | Discounts beyond this |
| We set it up for you and import your Excel list | "Works with your billing / hospital software" |
| Data is kept on servers in Singapore and Mumbai with daily encrypted backups; each business sees only its own data | "100% secure", "government approved" |
| Hospitals: names, phone numbers, appointments and follow-ups only | Storing prescriptions or medical records |

## 1. Opening

**English**
> Hello, am I speaking with [Dr. / Mr. / Ms.] [Name]? My name is [Your name], I'm calling from VBW, a software company here in [City].
> I'll take just one minute. Is this a good time?

*If no:* "No problem. When can I call back, today evening or tomorrow morning?" → note `next_step_date`.

**Tamil**
> வணக்கம், [டாக்டர் / சார் / மேடம்] [பெயர்] தானே பேசுறீங்க? என் பெயர் [உங்கள் பெயர்], [நகரம்]-ல இருக்கிற VBW software company-ல இருந்து பேசுறேன்.
> ஒரு நிமிஷம் தான் எடுத்துக்குவேன். இப்போ பேசலாமா?

*வேண்டாம் என்றால்:* "பரவாயில்லை சார். எப்போ கூப்பிடலாம்? இன்னைக்கு சாயங்காலமா, நாளைக்கு காலையிலயா?"

## 2. The reason for the call (pick the industry)

**Hospital — English**
> We've built a simple app for hospitals. It keeps track of every patient's next visit and follow-up, so no patient is forgotten.
> Can I ask: today, how do you remember which patients need to come back for a follow-up?

**Hospital — Tamil**
> Hospital-களுக்காக ஒரு simple app செஞ்சிருக்கோம். ஒவ்வொரு patient-ஓட அடுத்த visit, follow-up எல்லாத்தையும் ஞாபகம் வச்சுக்கும். எந்த patient-ஐயும் மறக்க மாட்டீங்க.
> ஒரு கேள்வி: இப்போ எந்த patient follow-up-க்கு வரணும்னு எப்படி ஞாபகம் வச்சுக்கிறீங்க?

**Gym — English**
> We've built a simple app for gyms. It shows whose membership is ending this week, so you can remind them before they stop coming.
> Can I ask: how do you keep track of membership renewals today?

**Gym — Tamil**
> Gym-களுக்காக ஒரு simple app செஞ்சிருக்கோம். இந்த வாரம் யாரோட membership முடியுதுன்னு காட்டும். அவங்க வராம நிக்கறதுக்கு முன்னாடியே remind பண்ணலாம்.
> இப்போ membership renewal எல்லாம் எப்படி track பண்றீங்க?

**Education — English**
> We've built a simple app for coaching centres. Every enquiry gets followed up, and you can see whose fee is due this week.
> Can I ask: when a parent enquires, how do you make sure someone calls them back?

**Education — Tamil**
> Coaching centre-களுக்காக ஒரு simple app செஞ்சிருக்கோம். வர்ற ஒவ்வொரு enquiry-க்கும் follow-up நடக்கும். இந்த வாரம் யாரோட fees கட்டணும்னும் தெரியும்.
> ஒரு parent enquiry பண்ணா, அவங்களுக்கு திரும்ப call பண்றதை எப்படி உறுதி பண்றீங்க?

## 4. The offer

**English**
> Here's what I'd like to offer. We're choosing a few [hospitals / gyms / centres] in [City] as pilot users.
> It's **free for 100 days**. We set it up for you and put your existing list into it. After that, if you find it useful,
> it's **₹599 a month plus GST** for you and one staff member, and that price stays the same for as long as you use it.
> If it's not useful, you just stop. No card, no commitment.

**Tamil**
> நான் சொல்ல வந்தது இதுதான். [நகரம்]-ல சில [hospitals / gyms / centres]-ஐ pilot-ஆ தேர்ந்தெடுக்கிறோம்.
> **100 நாள் முழுக்க free.** நாங்களே setup பண்ணி, உங்க இப்போதைய list-ஐ அதுல ஏத்தி தர்றோம். அதுக்கப்புறம் பயனுள்ளதா இருந்தா,
> நீங்களும் ஒரு staff-உம் சேர்ந்து **மாசம் ₹599 + GST** தான். நீங்க use பண்ற வரைக்கும் அந்த விலை மாறாது.
> பிடிக்கலைன்னா நிறுத்திடலாம். Card வேண்டாம், எந்த commitment-உம் இல்ல.

## 5. Ask for the meeting

**English**
> Can I come by for 15 minutes and show you on your own phone? Would [day] at [time] work, or is [other day] better?

**Tamil**
> 15 நிமிஷம் வந்து உங்க phone-லயே காட்டட்டுமா? [நாள்] [நேரம்] சரியா இருக்குமா, இல்ல [வேற நாள்] பரவாயில்லையா?

Always offer **two choices**. When they agree, repeat the day and time, then send the WhatsApp message in step 7.

## 8. At the visit (15 minutes)

1. Open VBW CRM on **their** phone browser. Sign them up together (2 minutes).
2. Pick their industry. Add 3 of **their** real upcoming follow-ups / renewals / enquiries.
3. Show the dashboard: "Every morning, open this and you'll know who to call."
4. Tap **WhatsApp** on one contact and show the ready message. **Do not send it.**
5. Ask for their Excel/register photo, and promise to import it within 24 hours.
6. Invite their staff member.
7. Set the 100-day pilot: run `activate --trial-days 100` (see [10 — Operations](10-operations.md)) and note `pilot_start`.
8. Fix a check-in call **7 days later** to see if they are using it.

## Filling the target list

[16-target-list-template.csv](16-target-list-template.csv) opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Delete the 3 EXAMPLE rows first.

| Column | What to put |
|---|---|
| `industry` | `hospital`, `gym`, `education`, `retail` or `real_estate` (aim for 100 rows, about 20 each) |
| `type` | For example dental, skin, physio, gym, yoga, coaching, tuition, skill training, clothing, jewellery, electronics, broker, builder |
| `phone`, `whatsapp`, `google_maps_link`, `rating`, `review_count` | Copied **by hand** from the business's Google Maps page |
| `status` | One of the values below |
| `call_attempts`, `last_call_date` | Update after every call (dates as YYYY-MM-DD) |
| `next_step`, `next_step_date` | What happens next and when. Every row that is not closed must have one. |
| `demo_date`, `pilot_start` | When the visit happened, and when the 100-day pilot started |
| `notes` | Their own words about their problem: you will use them in the demo |

Who to put first (a suggested starting rule, not a researched one; adjust after the first 30 calls): businesses with **20–300 Google reviews** (busy enough to need it, small enough to decide fast) and a mobile number on the listing.
Skip large hospitals and chains: they are not a fit ([02 — Customers and pricing](02-customers-and-pricing.md)).

Weekly numbers for [09 — Go-to-market](09-go-to-market.md): count rows by `status`.

## Status values for the target list

`new` → `called_no_answer` → `call_back` → `meeting_booked` → `pilot_started` → `active_user` → `paid`
or `not_interested` / `not_a_fit` (for example a large hospital).
