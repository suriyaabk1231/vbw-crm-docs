## Design notes

- Colours: the app's teal (`#0f766e`) for headings and the price box; WhatsApp green only for the WhatsApp button.
- One screenshot: the **phone dashboard** with demo data (made-up names only), next to "What you get".
- Fonts: one sans-serif, for example Inter or Noto Sans. For the Tamil lines use **Noto Sans Tamil** so the letters render correctly.
- Export two files: an **A4 PDF** for printing and a **1080 × 1920 PNG** for WhatsApp Status.
