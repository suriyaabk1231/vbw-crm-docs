# 2. Customers and pricing

| Industry | Examples | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Healthcare ("Hospital" in the app) | Small and mid-size hospitals, nursing homes, clinics, dental, skin, eye, physiotherapy practices | Front desk and follow-ups only. Large hospitals need hospital management systems with many staff and medical records |
| Fitness & wellness | Gyms, yoga studios, fitness centres | |
| Education | Coaching centres, tuition, skill-training institutes | |
| Retail | Clothing and saree shops, jewellery, electronics and mobiles, footwear, opticals, small supermarkets | Customer list, purchases, birthday and win-back offers ([26](26-industries-retail-real-estate.md)) |

Later candidates: Food & Beverage, Automotive, Professional Services, Manufacturing.

## The plan

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Price | **₹599 per account per month + GST** |
| People per account | **2** — one Owner and one Member (e.g. doctor + receptionist) |
| Devices | Each person: 1 phone + 1 browser at a time (a new login logs out the old one) |
| Storage | **20 GB per account**, shared by both people (how they split it is up to them) |
| File limits | Max 25 MB per file; PDF, images, Excel/CSV only |
| Free trial | **100 days** for every new account; ends exactly 100 days after sign-up, then view-only until paid (setting: `TRIAL_DAYS`) |
| After trial/plan ends | Account becomes **view-only**; data is never deleted |
| Paid separately later | WhatsApp API and SMS messages (prepaid wallet, phase 2+) |

## How customers pay

| Phase | How |
|---|---|
| **Phase 1 (now)** | Customer pays by **UPI or bank transfer** and messages us on WhatsApp. We activate their account with one command (see [Operations](10-operations.md)). |
| Phase 2 | Razorpay monthly subscription, paid online. Activation is automatic. |
