# 14 — 2-minute demo video script (English + Tamil)

Record **two videos**: one in Tamil, one in English, same screens. About 2 minutes each.
The demo is a **hospital**: it is the easiest story. At the end, one line each shows that gyms and coaching centres work the same way.
To make a version for one industry only, swap the words in the table at the bottom.

## Before recording

- Use a **demo account with made-up names**. Never show real patients' data.
  Sample names: Priya Lakshmi, Arun Kumar, Meena Raghavan, Karthik S, Fathima Begum, Suresh Babu.
- Prepare in the demo account: 6 patients; 3 appointments today; 2 follow-ups due this week (one overdue); 1 task.
- Record the **phone screen** (most owners will use a phone). Use the phone browser at full screen.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb, and fill the battery and Wi-Fi icons (or hide the status bar).
- Speak slowly. A pause is better than a rush. Add subtitles in the other language when editing.
- Final screen shows your WhatsApp number and the website, for 5 seconds.

## What not to show or say

- Do not press **Send** in WhatsApp on camera. Show the filled message, then go back.
- Do not say the app sends WhatsApp automatically, or that the phone reminds you (the Android app is not out yet).
- Do not show medical details. For hospitals we store only names, phone numbers, appointments and follow-ups.
