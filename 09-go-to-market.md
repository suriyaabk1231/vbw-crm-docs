**In one line:** find customers while the product launches — target **30 pilot businesses (10 per industry)**.
This is a target, not a prediction.

| Industry | One-line pitch |
|---|---|
| Hospitals | "Never miss a patient follow-up." |

## Pilot offer

Free for 100 days, then **₹599/month locked for life** (founder price). We set it up for them and import their Excel list.

## Numbers to track every week

| Number | Tells you |
|---|---|
| Businesses contacted | Sales effort |
| Pilot sign-ups | Interest |
| **Pilots using it every week** | Whether the product is useful (most important) |
| Pilots converted to paid | Willingness to pay |
| Cancellations | What to fix |
| Referrals | Customer happiness |

If pilots stop using it within 2 weeks, fix the product before selling more.
